# Suitability Studies Of Fruits Of West Indian elm Plant (Guazuma ulmifolia) As Feedstock For Biofuel production

Investigated Guazuma ulmifolia fruits as biofuel feedstock via petroleum ether solvent extraction, emulsion and acrolein lipid assays, and H2SO4 acid hydrolysis for fermentable sugar testing. No lipids detected; carbohydrates confirmed — supports bioethanol pathway.
