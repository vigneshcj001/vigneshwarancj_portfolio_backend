# Suitability Studies Of Fruits Of West Indian elm Plant (Guazuma ulmifolia) As Feedstock For Biofuel production

Investigated the potential of Guazuma ulmifolia (West Indian Elm) fruits as a feedstock for biofuel production. Fruits were ground into powder and extracted using solvent extraction with petroleum ether. Confirmatory tests for lipids (emulsion and acrolein assays) were performed on the solvent-vaporised residue. The processed biomass was subjected to H₂SO₄ acid hydrolysis followed by heat treatment and tested for fermentable sugars. The sample contained no lipids but did contain carbohydrates, supporting bioethanol potential over biodiesel. Future work focuses on comparative analysis of solvent extraction versus nano-catalysts and enzymatic pretreatment for improved sugar yield.
