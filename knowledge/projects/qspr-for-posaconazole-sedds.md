# QSPR for Posaconazole SEDDS

Developed QSPR models for Posaconazole solubility in Self-Emulsifying Drug Delivery Systems at Manipal Institute of Technology under Dr. Neha Sureshchandra Gandhi. Generated molecular descriptors with RDKit, reduced multicollinearity through VIF analysis, compared regression models, and reported model performance of R²=0.926.
