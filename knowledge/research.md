### Explainable Machine Learning-Based Approach to Developing Potent EGFR Inhibitors for Ovarian Cancer

- **Id:** egfr

- **Title:** Explainable Machine Learning-Based Approach to Developing Potent EGFR Inhibitors for Ovarian Cancer

- **Authors:** Vigneshwaran CJ

- **Institution:** SASTRA Deemed University

- **Year:** 2025

- **Status:** M.Tech Thesis

- **Type:** Thesis

- **Context:** PG M.Tech Big Data Biology · Thesis & Mini Project

- **Abstract:** M.Tech thesis developing an explainable ML framework for EGFR inhibitor discovery in ovarian cancer. More than 1,800 RDKit and Mordred descriptors were reduced through LASSO selection. Six classifiers were tuned using GridSearchCV and four-fold cross-validation. Random Forest reached 93% accuracy and tuned Gradient Boosting reached 98.28%. SHAP force and waterfall plots explained activity-driving descriptors.
